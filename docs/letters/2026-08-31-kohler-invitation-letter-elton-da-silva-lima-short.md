Dear Elton,

Prof. Hölscher spoke warmly of you, and since then Dr. Xydis has told me how much he has enjoyed working through your proposal with you. Now that I have read it myself, I wanted to write to you directly, because I did not want this invitation to feel like a formality.

Your project asks a question I find genuinely exciting: whether giving room-acoustic models a real sense of direction, rather than a single averaged number, changes what architects actually choose to build. It is ambitious, it is timely, and it is exactly the kind of question our group exists to help answer. Reading your proposal, I could see clearly how it grows out of work we already believe in, and how much you yourself would bring to it — not just as a good technical fit, but as someone who has clearly thought hard about why this question matters.

More than the science, though, what stayed with me was how much care went into this proposal, and how well it reflects who you are as a researcher. That matters to me just as much as the results it might produce. I would be proud to have you join Gramazio Kohler Research, to support your work here, to introduce you to the rest of the group, and to watch this project take shape over the coming years.

So please take this as a genuine, wholehearted invitation, not a polite formality. I would be delighted to host you as your ETH Fellow host professor, and I very much hope you will join us.

With best regards,

Matthias Kohler
